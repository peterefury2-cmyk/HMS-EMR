# Monetization Strategy

## SaaS Subscription Tiers

### Starter — $299/month
- Up to 5 doctors
- Up to 500 patients/month
- Core modules: Patients, EMR, Appointments, Billing
- Email support

### Professional — $799/month
- Up to 25 doctors
- Up to 2,500 patients/month
- All Starter modules + Pharmacy, Laboratory, Radiology, Insurance
- Telemedicine (100 sessions/month)
- Priority email & chat support

### Enterprise — Custom pricing
- Unlimited doctors and patients
- All modules including AI Engine
- Unlimited telemedicine
- Custom integrations & SLA
- Dedicated account manager
- On-premise deployment option

## Revenue Streams

1. **Monthly SaaS subscriptions** — Recurring MRR from all tiers
2. **Telemedicine overage** — Per-session fee above plan limit
3. **AI Engine usage** — Per-API-call pricing for heavy AI users
4. **Implementation services** — One-time setup and training fees
5. **White-labeling** — Enterprise clients can re-brand the platform
6. **Data analytics reports** — Premium dashboards and exports
7. **Insurance integrations** — Per-claim processing fee

## Payment Processing

- **Stripe** for subscription billing and one-time payments
- Invoicing via Stripe Billing
- PCI-DSS compliant (Stripe handles card data)
